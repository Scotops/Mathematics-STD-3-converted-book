Addition of Tanzanian currency

Adding currency is the same as adding whole numbers. When adding currency, consider that one shilling is equal to hundred cents.

Example 1

shs 500  20 cts+shs 380 70 cts=

Steps

1. Add cents: 20 cts + 70 cts = 90 cts, Write 90 in the cents place.
2.
Add shillings: shs 500 + shs 380 = shs 880. Write 880 in the shillings place.

Therefore, the answer is shs 880 90 cts.
