Chapter Five

Fractions

Introduction

In this chapter you will divide objects or group of objects to learn fractions. In addition, you will add and subtract fractions with the same denominator. The competencies developed will enable you to divide different things such as medicine, food and money as well as adding or subtracting fractions.

Recognising fractions

A fraction is a part of a thing that makes up a whole thing. When you cut an orange into slices and share it with your friends, each piece is called a fraction of the orange. Examples of fractions are 12, 13 or 25. The upper number is called the numerator while the lower one is called the denominator.
