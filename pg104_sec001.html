Mathematics Pupil’s Book Standard Three

A ray is represented by the symbol → on the top of its notation. For example, the ray AB is written as AB→, and the ray BA is written as BA→.

Activity 3: Drawing a ray

  1. 1.
    Use a ruler to draw a line segment AB.
  2. 2.
    Extend the line at any point and put an arrow-head to show the direction.
  3. 3.
    Repeat tasks 1 and 2 to draw three more rays and name them.
Example 1
What is the endpoint of a ray AC?
Answer

Since the point of origin of a ray is also called its endpoint.Therefore, the end point of the ray AC is point A.
