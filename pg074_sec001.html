Subtracting whole numbers vertically

Example 1
6 854
− 3 887
Steps

1. Subtract the ones: 4 − 7; 4 is insufficient in subtracting 7.

2. Thus, take 1 group of tens from 5 tens.

3. Regroup it to get 10 tens then add 4 ones: 10 + 4 = 14.

4. Now, 14 − 7 = 7.

5. Write 7 in the ones place.

6. Remember, 1 tens was regrouped into 10 ones.

7. Thus, 4 tens remained in the tens place.
