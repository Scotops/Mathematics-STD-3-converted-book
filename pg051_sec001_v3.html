Chapter Two: Addition and subtraction of whole numbers

Introduction

In this chapter, you will learn about addition and subtraction of whole numbers. You will add and subtract whole numbers aligned horizontally and vertically. You will also learn how to solve word problems involving addition and subtraction of whole numbers. The competencies developed will enable you to find sums or differences of various objects.

Addition of whole numbers

Whole numbers can be added without regrouping or by regrouping, depending on the nature of the numbers being added.

Addition of whole numbers without regrouping

When adding numbers, the place values of digits in the number should be considered. Start by adding ones, tens and so on.
