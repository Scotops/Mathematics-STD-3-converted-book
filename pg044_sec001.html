Activity

Use online programmes to learn more on whole numbers.

Descriptions

Explore how to do the following:

  • (a)Read and write whole numbers in numerals and in words
  • (b)Find the place values and total values of whole numbers
  • (c)Write whole numbers in expanded form
  • (d)Formulate sequence of whole numbers
  • (e)Compare the values of whole numbers

Summary

  1. 1.Numbers can be written in two different ways, that is, in numerals and in words.
  2. 2.When reading numbers, the place value and total value of a digit must be taken into consideration.
  3. 3.The equality symbol (=), greater than (>), and smaller than (<) are used to compare the relationship of the values of different objects or numbers.
  4. 4.A sequence of numbers always follows a certain pattern.
  5. 5.It is important to find out the operation involved in a given sequence in order to get a correct pattern.

Vocabulary

Inequality symbols
These are the symbols “smaller than” (<) “greater than” (>)
Sequence
Numbers arranged in a defined pattern
