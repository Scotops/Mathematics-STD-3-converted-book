Word problems involving addition of fractions

Example 1

Yona filled 39 of a tank with water, and Halima filled 29 of the tank. What fraction of the tank did they fill?

Solution

Yona filled 39 of the tank.

Halima filled 29 of the tank.

Both of them filled:
39+29=3+29
=59

Therefore, both Yona and Halima filled 59 of the tank with water.

Example 2

A household had one bag of sugar, Anna used 38 of the sugar and John used 48 of the sugar in the bag. What fraction of the sugar was used by both Anna and John?

Solution

Anna used 38 of the sugar and John used 48 of the sugar.

In total they used:
38+48=3+48
=78

Therefore, Anna and John used 78 of the sugar.
