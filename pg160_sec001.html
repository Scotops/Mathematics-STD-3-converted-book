Example 1
How do you differentiate a one hundred Tanzanian coin from other Tanzanian coins?
Answer

Each coin can be differentiated based on the number and features it contains. For example, the one hundred coin is written 100 on one of its sides. It also shows four running gazelles.

Example 2
What is the colour of the Tanzanian fifty shillings coin?
Answer

The Tanzanian fifty shillings coin is coated with the gold colour.

Example 3
Which coin has a picture of the first President of Tanzania?
Answer

It is the one hundred shillings coin.
