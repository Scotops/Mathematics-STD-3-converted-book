Mathematics Pupil’s Book Standard Three

Activity 3: Measuring the length of an exercise book

  1. 1. Measure the length of your exercise book in centimetres using a ruler.

  2. 2. Record the length measured in task 1.

  3. 3. Repeat task 1 by using a tape measure.

  4. 4. Record the length measured in task 3.

  5. 5. Compare the measurements obtained in task 3 and 4.

  6. 6. Explain your observation from task 5?
