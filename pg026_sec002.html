Writing whole numbers
A whole number is written from left to right by considering the place values of the digits. It can be written in numerals or in words. A number in numerals shows all the digits in it and their corresponding place values.
Example 1
Write the following numbers in words:
(a)1000
(b)1001
(c)1002
(d)1003
(e)1004
(f)1005
(g)1006
(h)1007
(i)1008
(j)1009
(k)1010
