Example 2

Read each of the following numbers:

(a) 1350
(b) 1572
(c) 9700
(d) 9540
(e) 9998

Answer

(a) 1350 is read as one thousand three hundred and fifty.
(b) 1572 is read as sixteen thousand five hundred and seventy-two.
(c) 9700 is read as nine thousand and seven hundred.
(d) 9540 is read as nine thousand five hundred and forty.
(e) 9998 is read as nine thousand nine hundred and ninety.
