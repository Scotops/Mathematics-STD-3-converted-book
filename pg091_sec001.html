Activity 6: Measuring the volume of water

1. Take two empty containers of the same size.
2. Counting the number of cups, fill one container with water.
3. Ask your friend to do task 2 by using the same measuring tools used in task 2.
4. Record the number of cups used to fill your container and that of your friend.
