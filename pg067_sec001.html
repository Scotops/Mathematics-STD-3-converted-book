Example 1
6874 - 2430 =
Steps
1.

Subtract the ones: 4 - 0 = 4 Write 4 in the ones place.

2.

Subtract the tens: 7 - 3 = 4 Write 4 in the tens place.

3.

Subtract the hundreds: 8 - 4 = 4. Write 4 in the hundreds place.
