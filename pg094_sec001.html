Activity 8: Investigate different measuring tools in our daily lives

1.Visit a nearby shop in your environment where people sell different items.
2.Use accessible tools. Observe / experience / note / explore the way people measure lengths, masses and volumes of different items.
3.Identify two items from the list of your observation.
4.Which tools do people use to measure the lengths, masses and volumes of the identified items in task 3?
5.Record the lengths, masses and volumes of the identified items.
Activity 9: Learn more about measurements from online sources

Summary

1.

The basic unit of length is metre (m).

2.

The basic unit of mass is kilogram (kg).

3.

The basic unit of volume is litre (L).

4.

The relationship between metre and centimetre is 1 mL = 100 cm.

5.

The relationship between kilograms and grams is 1 kg = 1000 g.

6.

The relationship between litre and millilitre is 1 L = 1000 mL.
