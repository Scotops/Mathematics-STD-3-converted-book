Activity 8: Investigate different measuring tools in our daily lives

1.Visit a nearby shop in your environment where people sell different items.
2.Observe the way people measure lengths, masses and volumes of different items.
3.Identify two items from the list of your observation.
4.Which tools do people use to measure the lengths, masses and volumes of the identified items in task 3?
5.Record the lengths, masses and volumes of the identified items.
