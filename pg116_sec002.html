Activity 1: Recognising fractions

Activity 1: Recognising fractions

Steps

1.

Take a knife, marker pen, and an orange or watermelon.

2.

Use a knife to cut the orange or watermelon into several equal pieces. Start with halves or quarters.

3.

Count the number of slices obtained in step 2.

4.

Use a marker pen to label each slice with its corresponding fraction.

5.

Repeat steps 2, 3, and 4 to cut an orange into different fractions. For example cut it into quarters, thirds or fifths.
