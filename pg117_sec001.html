Example 2

A mother gave mangoes to her three children as follows: Rashid got 2 mangoes, Irene got 2 mangoes, and Likweme got 2 mangoes. What fraction of mangoes did Rashid get?

Solution
Rashid got 2 mangoes
Irene got 2 mangoes
Likweme got 2 mangoes
Total mangoes: 2+2+2=6

Therefore, Rashid got 26 of the mangoes.
