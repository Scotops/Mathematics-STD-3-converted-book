Exercise 5

1.

Half of Ngosha’s farm is cultivated. What fraction of his farm is not cultivated?

2.

Aisha harvested oranges from her farm. If a quarter of the oranges were spoiled, what part of the oranges were not spoiled?

3.

Bahati divided a watermelon into 10 equal pieces. If 7 out of the 10 pieces were sold, what fraction of the watermelon was not sold?

4.

Mariam ate one-sixths of a whole sugarcane. What fraction of the sugarcane remained?

5.

Kandi had 59 of a loaf of bread. He gave Ali 49 of the bread. What part of the bread did he remain with?

6.

Kabula answered 712 of Mathematics questions in the morning and 212 in the afternoon. What fraction of the questions remained?
