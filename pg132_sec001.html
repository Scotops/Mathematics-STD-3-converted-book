Summary

1.

Some fractions of shapes have special names; for example, 14 is called a quarter, 12 is a half, and 13 is a third.

2.

The number above the division line is called numerator and the one below the line is called denominator.

3.

Adding and subtracting fractions with the same denominator, the denominator does not change.
