Summary
1.
When adding or subtracting numbers, align the numbers either horizontally or vertically. Start by adding the ones, followed by the tens, then the hundreds digits and so on.
2.
When adding or subtracting numbers, always start from the rightmost digit and end with the leftmost digit.
3.
In solving word problems, consider the following:
(a)
Read the problem to identify the mathematical operations involved.
(b)
Perform the operations on the values mentioned in the problem.
(c)
Conclude by answering the question.
