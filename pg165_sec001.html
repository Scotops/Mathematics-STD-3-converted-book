Example 1

(a) How many 50 cents are there in 1 shilling?

(b) Write 500 shillings and 50 cents in short form.

Solution

(a) 100 cents = 1 shilling. Since 100 cts = 50 cts + 50 cts and sh 1 = 100 cts = 50 cts + 50 cts, it follows that, sh 1 is the same as twice 50 cts.

Therefore, there are two 50 cents in 1 shilling.

(b) In short form it is written as shs 500 and 50 cts.

Example 2

(a) Write shs 700 in words.

(b) Write in short form shs 1200 and 70 cts.

Answer

(a) shs 700 in words is written as seven hundred shillings.

(b) shs 1200 and 70 cts is written in short form as shs 1200 70 cts.
