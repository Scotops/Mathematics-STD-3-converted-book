Addition of whole numbers by regrouping

Adding whole numbers horizontally

Example 1

6327 + 3496 =

Solution

Add the digits from right to left.

Steps
1.

Add the ones: 7 + 6 =13. Write 3 in the ones place. Regroup 10 ones into 1 tens and shift it to the tens place.

2.

Add the tens: 1 + 2 + 9 = 12. Write 2 in the tens place. Regroup 10 tens into 1 hundreds and shift it to the hundreds place.

3.

Add the hundreds: 1 + 3 + 4 = 8. Write 8 in the hundreds place.
