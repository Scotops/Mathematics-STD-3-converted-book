Example 3
Fill in the missing numbers in the following sequence:
25, 23, 21, ___, ___
Solution

This sequence shows that the numbers decrease by 2 in each step.

Thus,
25 - 2 = 23
23 - 2 = 21
21 - 2 = 19
19 - 2 = 17
Therefore, the answer is 25, 23, 21, 19, 17.
