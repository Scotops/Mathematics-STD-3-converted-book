Reading and writing whole numbers

Reading and writing whole numbers involves interpreting numerical symbols and expressing quantities in written form. In this section, you will learn the techniques for reading and writing whole numbers.

Reading whole numbers

Reading whole numbers depends on the number of digits and the total value of the digits in the corresponding number. Start identifying the ones, tens, hundreds, thousands, and so on. Thereafter, read the number from left to right by considering the total value of the corresponding digit. That is, read a number beginning with a digit that has the highest place value.

Example 1
Read the following numbers:
(a) 1001
(b) 1086
(c) 1100
Answer

(a) 1001 is read as one thousand and one. (b) 1086 is read as one thousand and eighty-six. (c) 1100 is read as one thousand and one hundred.
