Comparing whole numbers

Whole numbers can be compared when they are equal, greater than, or less than another. When we have two whole numbers or quantities to compare, we use three basic symbols, which are “equality symbol” (=), “greater than” (>), and “less than” (<).

Example
Use the symbols <, = and > to compare the following numbers:
(a) 8900 8090
(b) 3220 3220
(c) 3240 3420
(d) 715 517
Answer
(a) 8900 > 8090
(b) 3220 = 3220
(c) 3240 < 3420
(d) 715 > 517
