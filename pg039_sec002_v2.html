Example 1
Write the missing numbers in the following sequence:
1, 4, 7, 10, 13, 16, 19, 22, 25.
Solution
In this sequence, the numbers are increasing by 3 in each step:
Thus,
1 + 3 = 4
4 + 3 = 7
7 + 3 = 10
10 + 3 = 13
13 + 3 = 16
16 + 3 = 19
19 + 3 = 22
22 + 3 = 25
Therefore, the answer is 1, 4, 7, 10, 13, 16, 19, 22, 25.
