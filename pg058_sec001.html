Add the digits from right to left.

Steps
1.

Add the ones: 1 + 9 = 10. Write 0 in the ones place. Regroup 10 ones into 1 tens and shift it to the tens place.

2.

Add the tens: 1 + 7 + 8 = 16. Write 6 in the tens place. Regroup 10 tens into 1 hundreds and shift it to the hundreds place.

3.

Add the hundreds: 1 + 2 + 9 = 12. Write 2 in the hundreds place. Regroup 10 hundreds into 1 thousands and shift it to the thousands place.

4.

Add the thousands: 1 + 8 + 2 = 11. Write 1 in the thousands place. Regroup 10 thousands into 1 ten thousands and shift it to the ten thousands place.

5.

Add the ten thousands: 1 + 5 + 3 = 9. Write 9 in the ten thousands place.
