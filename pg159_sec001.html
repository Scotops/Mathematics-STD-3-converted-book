Introduction

In this chapter, you will learn about the Tanzanian currency in shillings and cents. Also, you will add and subtract Tanzanian currency in shillings and cents. The competencies developed will enable you to use the Tanzanian currency in purchasing, budgeting, managing, and saving money.

Tanzanian currency

Tanzanian currency is in the form of shillings and cents. The value of one cent is smaller than the value of one shilling. One shilling is equal to one hundred cents. A cent is mostly observed in writing than its availability in everday use. Also, Tanzanian currency with different values are in the form of coins and banknotes.

Tanzanian coin

Tanzanian coins have different deniminations. In the circulation there are fifty, one hundred, two hundred and five hundred shillings coins. Tanzanian coins show various features including, faces of presidents, wildlife, historical monuments, and national symbols.
