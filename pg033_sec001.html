Example 3

Write the place value of each digit in 9261514, then write the number in words.

Solution
4 is in ones
1 is in tens
5 is in hundreds
1 is in thousands
6 is in ten thousands
2 is in hundred thousands
9 is in millions

Therefore, 9261514 is read as nine million two hundred sixty-one thousand five hundred and fourteen.
