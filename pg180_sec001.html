10. Fabian bought a television set for 435,250 Tanzanian shillings. What profit did he make after selling the television set at 582,950 Tanzanian shillings?

Activity:

Learning Tanzanian currency through online programmes

Instruction:

Use online programmes to learn addition and subtraction of shillings and cents.

Summary

1.

When you separate shillings and cents in digits, cents are written as two digits.

2.

One hundred cents is equal to one shilling.

3.

When adding and subtracting currency, start with cents and then shillings.

4.

Tanzanian currency is in the form of coins and banknotes with different values.

5.

Money is used for making payments. In addition, it shows the value of things in the form of prices.

Vocabulary

Banknote

a piece of special paper representing a particular value of money

Coin

a piece of metal representing a particular value of money
