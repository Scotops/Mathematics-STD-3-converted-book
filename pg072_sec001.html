Subtraction of whole numbers by regrouping

Subtracting whole numbers horizontally

Example 1
5371 - 3282 =

Steps

1. Subtract the ones: 1 – 2; 1 is insufficient in subtracting 2. Thus, take 1 group of tens from 7 tens. Regroup it to get 10 ones then add 1 ones: 10 + 1 =11. Now, 11 – 2 = 9. Write 9 in the ones place. Remember, 1 tens was regrouped into 10 ones. Thus, 6 tens remained in the tens place.

2. Subtract the tens: 6 – 8; 6 is insufficient in subtracting 8. Thus, take 1 group of hundreds from 3 hundreds. Regroup it to get 10 tens then add 6 tens: 10 + 6 = 16. Now, it becomes 16 – 8 = 8. Write 8 in the tens place. Remember, 1 hundreds was regrouped into 10 tens. Thus, 2 hundreds remained in the hundreds place.

3. Subtract the hundreds: 2 – 2 = 0. Write 0 in the hundreds place.

4. Subtract the thousands: 5 – 3 = 2. Write 2 in the thousands place.

Therefore, 5371 – 3282 = 2089.
