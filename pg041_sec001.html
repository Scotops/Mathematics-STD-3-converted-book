Example 5

Fill in the missing number in the following sequence: 45, 41, 37, 33, ____

Solution

In this sequence, the numbers are decreasing by 4 in each step. Thus,

45 - 4 = 41
41 - 4 = 37
37 - 4 = 33
33 - 4 = 29

Therefore, the answer is 45, 41, 37, 33, 29.
