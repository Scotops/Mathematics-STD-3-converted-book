Summary

1.

Two dimensional figures are such as squares, rectangles, triangles, and hexagons.

2.

A square has sides which are equal in length, while a rectangle has pairs of opposite sides with equal length.

3.

A line segment is drawn by joining two points.

4.

A ray has an initial point and extends one side infinitely.

Vocabulary

Hexagona plane figure enclosed by six sides
Rectanglea four-sided figure whose opposite sides are equal in length
Squarea four-sided figure whose sides are equal in length
