Activity: My day in clocks

Instructions: Use accessible tools and basic drawing software (for example, Paint) to create a digital timetable illustrating your daily activities. Draw both analogue and digital clock faces to depict the times you engage in each activity.

Summary

1.
On the clock face, the short arrow shows hours, the long arrow shows minutes.
2.
One hour is equal to 60 minutes.
3.
Time can be measured by counting number of days, weeks, month or year
